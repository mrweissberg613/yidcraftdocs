## 1. Be Respectful
No harassment, including building harassment, and no targeting players.

## 2. Keep It Family Friendly
No swearing, sexual talk, offensive jokes, or inappropriate names, signs, books, or builds.

## 3. Zero Tolerance for Hate
No racism, antisemitism, sexism, homophobia, or slurs.

## 4. No Spam or Advertising
No flooding chat, abusing caps, or promoting other servers.

## 5. No Threats or Doxxing
Never threaten players or share anyone's personal information.

## 6. No Cheating
No hacked clients, X-ray, macros, auto clickers, unauthorized mods, or exploits.

<Aside type="note">
  Minimap mods are allowed, but cave mode and entity radar must be turned off.
</Aside>

## 7. No Unfair Farming
No duplication, automatic toggle farms, or AFK abuse.

## 8. No Lag Machines
No lag machines, chunk loaders, or stasis chambers.

## 9. Fight Fair
- No spawn killing or camping near spawn.
- No combat logging.
- Spawn killing includes killing players at their respawn point, such as at a home or bed.

## 10. Trade Honestly
No scamming, and no real-money trading of items, currency, accounts, or services.

## 11. No Griefing
Do not grief other players' builds or property.

## 12. Respect Staff
Follow staff instructions. Do not impersonate or mislead staff.
