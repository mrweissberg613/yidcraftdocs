# Server Rules

<Aside type="tip" title="Welcome!">
YidCraft is a **family-friendly, Jewish-themed Minecraft survival server** built around respect, fairness, and community. By joining the server, you agree to follow these rules. Violations may result in warnings, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans depending on the severity of the offense.
</Aside>

---

## Community Conduct

### 1.1 Be Respectful

Treat every player with kindness and respect.

The following behavior is **not allowed**:

- Bullying
- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Targeted toxicity
- Intentionally ruining another player's experience

<Aside type="note" title="Friendly Reminder">
Friendly jokes and banter are welcome, but if another player asks you to stop, please respect their wishes.
</Aside>

---

### 1.2 Family-Friendly Chat

YidCraft is designed to be enjoyable for players of all ages.

The following are **not allowed** anywhere on the server:

- Swearing or profanity
- Sexual or inappropriate discussions
- Offensive jokes
- Inappropriate usernames
- Inappropriate nicknames
- Inappropriate signs
- Offensive books
- Offensive item names

<Aside type="caution" title="Keep It Clean">
If you wouldn't say it around young children or in school, don't say it on YidCraft.
</Aside>

---

### 1.3 No Discrimination

We have **zero tolerance** for hate speech.

This includes:

- Racism
- Antisemitism
- Sexism
- Homophobia
- Religious discrimination
- Slurs of any kind

<Aside type="danger" title="Zero Tolerance">
Serious discrimination or hate speech may result in an immediate permanent ban.
</Aside>

---

### 1.4 No Spam

Keep chat enjoyable for everyone.

Examples include:

- Repeating messages
- Flooding chat
- Excessive capital letters
- Meaningless characters
- Advertising unrelated servers or communities

---

### 1.5 No Threats

The following are strictly prohibited:

- Threatening players
- Doxxing
- DDoS threats
- Sharing personal information
- Encouraging real-life harm

<Aside type="danger" title="Immediate Action">
Threats involving real-life harm or personal information will result in severe punishment, including permanent bans when appropriate.
</Aside>

---

## Player Interaction

### 2.1 Respect Other Players

Do not intentionally target another player simply to ruin their experience.

Examples include:

- Constantly following a player
- Repeatedly killing the same player
- Preventing someone from enjoying normal gameplay
- Harassing a player after they ask you to stop

---

### 2.2 Fair PvP

PvP is allowed where enabled by the server.

However, you may **not**:

- Exploit unintended mechanics
- Spawn kill players
- Abuse combat bugs
- Trap players unfairly
- Force players into PvP using exploits

---

### 2.3 Respect Staff

Staff members are here to help keep the server fair.

Please do not:

- Ignore staff instructions
- Argue about punishments in public chat
- Impersonate staff members
- Harass staff members

If you disagree with a punishment, contact staff privately.

---

## Fair Gameplay

### 3.1 No Cheating

Using unfair advantages is prohibited.

Examples include:

- Hacked clients
- X-Ray
- Auto-clickers
- Combat macros
- Duplication glitches
- Unauthorized client modifications
- Exploit abuse

<Aside type="danger" title="Play Fair">
Using cheats or hacked clients may result in an immediate ban.
</Aside>

---

### 3.2 Report Bugs

Found a bug?

<Steps>

1. Stop using the bug immediately.
2. Report it to a staff member.
3. Wait for further instructions.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Help Improve YidCraft">
Reporting bugs instead of abusing them helps keep the server fair for everyone.
</Aside>

---

### 3.3 No Automated Totem Farming

Automated Totem of Undying farms are not allowed.

Totems are intended to be earned through normal gameplay.

---

### 3.4 No AFK Abuse

Do not use AFK methods or automation to gain unfair advantages or bypass intended gameplay mechanics.

---

## Economy & Trading

### 4.1 No Scamming

All trades must be completed honestly.

The following are prohibited:

- Item switching during trades
- Misrepresenting items
- Deceptive trades
- Abusing shop mechanics

---

### 4.2 No Duplication

Duplicating items, money, or exploiting economy bugs is strictly prohibited.

---

### 4.3 No Real-World Trading

Do not buy or sell:

- Items
- In-game currency
- Accounts
- Services

for real-world money unless officially authorized by YidCraft.

---

## Builds

### 5.1 Appropriate Builds

Builds must remain appropriate for a family-friendly community.

The following are prohibited:

- Offensive builds
- Hate symbols
- Inappropriate pixel art
- Explicit or sexual content

---

### 5.2 No Lag Machines

Do not intentionally build machines designed to create excessive lag or crash the server.

---

### 5.3 Respect Other Builds

Do not grief, steal from, or intentionally damage another player's property unless the server's gameplay specifically allows it.

---

## Jewish Community Guidelines

### 6.1 Shabbat & Holidays

YidCraft may temporarily close during:

- Shabbat
- Yom Tov

We encourage everyone to:

- Spend time with family
- Rest
- Disconnect from technology
- Focus on real life

<Aside type="note" title="Why?">
Observing Shabbat and Jewish holidays is part of what makes YidCraft unique.
</Aside>

---

### 6.2 Derech Eretz

"דֶּרֶךְ אֶרֶץ" (*Derech Eretz*) means treating others with dignity and respect.

On YidCraft this means:

- Be patient
- Be kind
- Welcome new players
- Speak respectfully
- Help others whenever possible

---

### 6.3 Honesty & Integrity

Always play fairly and honestly.

Do **not**:

- Cheat
- Scam
- Exploit bugs
- Mislead staff
- Gain unfair advantages

Honest players help make YidCraft a welcoming community for everyone.

---

<Aside type="tip" title="Need Help?">
Not sure if something is allowed? Ask a staff member before doing it. We'd much rather answer a question than issue a punishment
</Aside>
