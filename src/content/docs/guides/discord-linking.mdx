The first time you join, you do not land in YidCraft straight away. You land in a small **waiting room** with a parkour course. You stay there until your Minecraft account is linked to your Discord account. It only takes a minute.

When you arrive, a menu opens with four cards: **Link Discord**, **About YidCraft**, **Rules** and **Tutorial**. Click a card to open it. Your link code is in chat behind the menu: close it with **Play parkour** (or Escape) to see your code. `/welcome` opens the menu again.

## Link your account

<Steps>

1. **Join our Discord.**

   You must be in our Discord server, and stay in it, to play. Join here: [discord.gg/yVacYbMEqH](https://discord.gg/yVacYbMEqH)

2. **Join the server and find your code.**

   Join `yidcraft.jewishgaming.net` (see [How to join](/guides/how-to-join/)). A short **code** appears in your chat. Keep it.

   Lost it, or it expired? Leave and join again, and a new code appears.

3. **Send the code, one of two ways.**

   **Way 1: send a DM to our bot.** The bot is called **Yid Craft**. Open a DM with it and send just the code, nothing else.

   <img src="/assets/discord-linking-bot.png" alt="The Yid Craft bot's profile in Discord" width="320" />

   To find the bot, look for **Yid Craft** in the member list of our Discord. Or paste this in any Discord chat, click the name it turns into, and choose **Message**:

   ```text
   <@1490778291467063368>
   ```

   **Way 2: the Link Account button.** Open the [link channel](https://discord.com/channels/1405767136579682415/1516159525827252244) and click **Link Account**. The bot asks for your code: type it in that channel within 60 seconds.

   <img src="/assets/discord-linking-panel.png" alt="The Link your account panel with its Link Account button" width="700" />

4. **Come in.**

   When the bot says your account is linked, leave and join again. You go straight to YidCraft.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Why it can take a while">
We check every new player before they come in, to keep YidCraft safe from griefers and from antisemitic attacks. A real person reads each application, so it can take some time. Please be patient: trust is worth the wait, and it is what keeps YidCraft a kind and safe place.
</Aside>

## While you wait

Three people in the waiting room are glad to talk to you. Right click any of them.

- **Reb Shimon the Shamash** greets you when you arrive and walks you through linking your account.
- **Reb Tuvia**, who runs the tutorial on YidCraft, tells you what he will teach you once you are in. The **Tutorial** card takes you straight to him.
- **Ari**, Reb Tuvia's talmid, has a short guide to the world: Shekels, kosher food and brachos, your home and mezuzah, sefarim and skills, the town and its bosses, and Shabbos.

Next to Ari is a row of real YidCraft items, turning slowly, so you know them when you see them: a **mezuzah**, a **sefer**, a **shechita knife**, **challah**, a **Gold Shekel** and a **kippah**.

<Aside type="note" title="A small download">
The waiting room asks you to accept a tiny resource pack, so the menu's pictures and those items show as they really look. YidCraft has its own, bigger pack, which it asks for when you get in.
</Aside>

## When you get in: do the tutorial

Find **Reb Tuvia** by the fountain at spawn. His [tutorial](/guides/tutorial/) teaches everything in short scenes, and pays **100 Shekels** when you finish. We really recommend doing it before anything else.

## On Bedrock

Everything works the same. The menu is a window with four buttons, the guides show their pages in a window too, and links are written out in brackets so you can type them. Your name in game starts with a dot, like `.YourName`. More on [Playing on Bedrock](/guides/bedrock/).

## Common questions

### The bot says my code is invalid or expired.

Codes only last a short while. Leave the server and join again for a new code, then send that one.

### I linked, but I am still in the waiting room.

Leave and join again. Linking does not move you by itself.

### I left the Discord and now I cannot play.

You must stay in our Discord to play. Join it again, then rejoin the server.
