## Bedrock Edition

YidCraft is on Bedrock: phone, tablet, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and Windows. Add the server with the address `yidcraft.jewishgaming.net` and port `21702`. Menus like `/pets` and the Boss Journal open as Bedrock windows, and you can link your Java and Bedrock accounts to play as one. [Read more](/guides/bedrock/)

## Boss Hunting

Six legendary bosses from the Gemara and Midrash, each with its own quest, its own story, and its own arena far out in the world: the **False Scholar** in the Silent Library, the **Golem** in the Clay Pits, **Og, King of Bashan** on his hills, the **Ziz** on its mountain peak, the **Leviathan** in its bay, and **Behemoth** on the Thousand Hills. Talk to Reb Yehoshua the Archivist in the town square to start, bring friends, and fill your **Boss Journal** (`/bosses`). [Read more](/features/bosses/)

## Pets

23 pets to collect: buy one at **Reb Zelig Katz**'s stall by the pond, tame a wild one with a **Keeper's Treat**, or be the lucky one a boss pet follows home. They walk with you or wait at home, each does one small kindness, and you can sell one to a friend with `/pets sell`. [Read more](/features/pets/)

## The Tutorial

**Reb Tuvia** stands by the fountain at spawn. Click him to visit his island and learn everything through short scenes, and finish every section for 100 Shekels. [Read more](/guides/tutorial/)

## The Town

New residents to meet: a baker, a sheitelmacher, a carpenter, a florist, the Judaica shop, the Beis Din and mikveh receptions, the gabbai, the pet keeper and Homeless Yankel. Many run a shop, several ask a weekly favour, and the mikveh reception lets you in for 50 Shekels. [Read more](/features/town/)

## Player Shops

Buy an NPC-run business that trades while you are offline. Stock its shelves, set prices, post buy orders, collect earnings and sell the shop to another player later. [Read more](/features/chestshops/)

## Kashrus

- **Pas Yisroel**: baked goods remember who baked them.
- **Cholov Yisroel**: milk remembers who milked it.
- **Cake is milchig** now, and remembers both.
- **Brachos in chat**: the right bracha before and after you eat, only for you.
