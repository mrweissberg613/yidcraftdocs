## Limbo: your first join

New players now wait in **limbo** while they link their Discord account. **Reb Shimon the Shamash** shows you how, step by step, and **Ari** tells you about the world, with a few real YidCraft items to look at. [Read more](/guides/limbo/)

## Find your teammates

`/team mark <player>` points the way to a teammate on your action bar, and `/team glow` makes your whole team glow through walls. Only you see either one. [Read more](/features/teams/#finding-your-teammates)

## Bosses

- **A boss left alone heals.** Leave the arena, or stop hitting it, and it slowly recovers. Keep the pressure on.
- **Every boss has a weak spot.** Find the weapon it fears most.
- **The Ziz flies lower,** so it is easier to reach.
- **Og and the Leviathan can be hit again.** Hits on them were blocked with "you can't harm that here".
- **Woke a boss and logged off?** Your waking item is waiting when you log back in.

[Read more](/features/bosses/)

## Sefarim

Sefarim are rarer now, in chests, from bosses and in the Chitas Crate. A sefer for a skill you already know is no longer used up when you open it. [Read more](/features/skills/)

## Mezuzos

A worn out mezuzah no longer protects the door. Put a new one up on the same doorway and the old one comes down into your pack. [Read more](/features/mezuzos/#wear-and-repair)

## Random teleport, rules and a combat tag

`/rtp` sends you somewhere new for Shekels, `/rules` shows the rules in game, and `/ping` shows your connection. Hitting a player now tags you both for 30 seconds: no teleporting away, and do not log off. [Basics](/guides/basics/#random-teleport) and [Rules](/guides/rules/#combat-tag)

## More trivia

Over 80 questions now, and none repeats until all have been asked. [Read more](/features/achievements/)

## Tefillin, tzitzis and mivtzoim

Put on tefillin in three steps and type the brachos in chat, the way you say Modeh Ani. Wear a tallis katan, or tie tzitzis onto your chestplate or leggings. For women, a small gold Chai necklace. Jewish villagers now look the part: find one with `/mivtzoim` and help him put on tefillin, or give her Shabbos candles. Light the menorah on Chanukah. [Read more](/features/mitzvos/)

## Kippah, hats, tichel and sheitel

Four head coverings you can dye, wear, and put over any helmet for more protection: a **Diamond Black Hat** is a real thing now. [Read more](/features/headwear/)

## New sefarim

Hilchos Tefillin, Hilchos Tzitzis, Hilchos Kisui HaRosh, Melachas HaTzoref and Hilchos Asakim. Each teaches a craft, and Hilchos Asakim lets you search every player shop with `/shopsearch`. A sofer can now copy a sefer with Ink of Truth. [Read more](/features/skills/)

## The Chitas Pass

A free daily pass: a gift every day, three small tasks, and thirty tiers of rewards each month. Type `/chitas`. [Read more](/features/chitas/)

## Crates

Boss spoils now come in a crate that opens in front of you. Only players who really fought get one, and the rare boss pets are found inside. The drops also craft into useful things: a Clay Guardian, Behemoth's Fodder, Og's Iron Shield, Ziz Feather Boots and a Leviathan Scale Helmet. [Read more](/features/bosses/#crates)

## Trivia and achievements

A Torah trivia question in chat every 20 minutes, and twenty achievements for the mitzvos you keep doing. [Read more](/features/achievements/)

## New foods

Pomegranates, grapes, grape juice, wine from a barrel, and Ozen Haman. [Read more](/features/customfoods/)

## Kosher Points

Mitzvah Points are now Kosher Points. If you had any, they were added to your Kosher Points.

## Bedrock Edition

YidCraft is on Bedrock: phone, tablet, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and Windows. Add the server with the address `yidcraft.jewishgaming.net` and port `21702`. Menus like `/pets` and the Boss Journal open as Bedrock windows, and you can link your Java and Bedrock accounts to play as one. [Read more](/guides/bedrock/)

## Boss Hunting

Six legendary bosses from the Gemara and Midrash, each with its own quest, its own story, and its own arena far out in the world: the **False Scholar** in the Silent Library, the **Golem** in the Clay Pits, **Og, King of Bashan** on his hills, the **Ziz** on its mountain peak, the **Leviathan** in its bay, and **Behemoth** on the Thousand Hills. Talk to Reb Yehoshua the Archivist in the town square to start, bring friends, and fill your **Boss Journal** (`/bosses`). [Read more](/features/bosses/)

## Pets

23 pets to collect: buy one at **Reb Zelig Katz**'s stall by the pond, tame a wild one with a **Keeper's Treat**, or be the lucky one a boss pet follows home. They walk with you or wait at home, each does one small kindness, and you can sell one to a friend with `/pets sell`. [Read more](/features/pets/)

## The Tutorial

**Reb Tuvia** stands by the fountain at spawn. Click him to visit his island and learn everything through short scenes, and finish every section for 100 Shekels. [Read more](/guides/tutorial/)

## The Town

New residents to meet: a baker, a sheitelmacher, a carpenter, a florist, the Judaica shop, the Beis Din and mikveh receptions, the gabbai, the pet keeper and Homeless Yankel. Many run a shop, several ask a weekly favour, and the mikveh reception lets you in for 50 Shekels. [Read more](/features/town/)

## Player Shops

Buy an NPC-run business that trades while you are offline. Stock its shelves, set prices, post buy orders, collect earnings and sell the shop to another player later. [Read more](/features/chestshops/)
