---
title: Stick Recipe
description: How to craft sticks from planks.
---

<img src="/recipes/stick.png" alt="Stick crafting recipe: two oak planks stacked vertically make 4 sticks" width="400" height="182" />

Place **2 planks**, one directly above the other, in a crafting grid to get **4 sticks**. Any type of wood planks works, and the recipe fits in the 2×2 grid in your inventory, so you do not need a crafting table.
