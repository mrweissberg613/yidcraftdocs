New to YidCraft? **Reb Tuvia**, the guide, shows you how everything works, with short scenes you watch instead of walls of text.

## Finding him

<img src="/assets/tutorial-reb-tuvia.jpg" alt="Reb Tuvia the guide by the fountain at spawn" width="800" />

Reb Tuvia stands by the fountain at **spawn**. Right click him: the screen fades, and you are on his island, a quiet stage where there is room for his scenes. He greets you and opens the tutorial menu.

## The sections

<img src="/assets/tutorial-island.jpg" alt="Reb Tuvia's island, where the tutorial scenes play" width="800" />

Pick any section, in any order, and come back for the rest whenever you like.

| Section | What you learn |
|---|---|
| **Kosher Food** | What you can eat, what you can't, and why meat takes more work |
| **Shechita** | Toiveling the knife and the oven, then watching a shochet at work |
| **Brachos** | Modeh Ani in the morning, and the bracha on a mezuzah |
| **Mezuzah** | Putting one up, and who can open your door after |
| **Economy** | Shekels: earning them, keeping them, trading them |
| **Server Basics** | Getting home, teams, and what you already know |
| **Exploration** | The rare sefarim hidden out in the world |
| **Boss Hunting** | Reb Yehoshua, the quests he gives, and the bosses waiting out there |
| **Teams** | Playing with friends: team chat, a team home and shared doors |

Some sections end with a quick question. Pick an answer and the guide tells you whether you got it, and why.

After each section you get a link to its page on this wiki.

**Finish every section** and you earn **100 Shekels**, once.

While you are on the island, other players' chat waits for you, so nothing interrupts a scene. You get it all when you come back.

## Going back

<img src="/assets/tutorial-arch.jpg" alt="The stone arch on the island: walk through it to go back to town" width="800" />

- Choose **"I'm done for now"** in the menu, and a few seconds later you are back in town, in front of Reb Tuvia.
- Or walk through the **stone arch** past Reb Tuvia on the island, marked "Back to town". It takes you back whenever you like.
- Or type **`/tutorial leave`** at any point, even in the middle of a scene.

Talk to Reb Tuvia again whenever you want to carry on. He remembers which sections you have done.

`/tutorial wiki` sends you the link to this wiki.
