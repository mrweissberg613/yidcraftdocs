The Tzedakah system puts donated Shekels into one shared fund. Staff use that money to help new players get started on the server.

<img src="/assets/tzedakah-box.png" alt="A Tzedakah Box beside the Community Tzedakah fund display" width="800" />

## Donating at a Tzedakah Box

Find the **Tzedakah Box** at spawn. Right-click it. Each click donates **1 Shekel** from your balance, and the chest stays closed.

The hologram above the box shows how much the community has donated, how much is left in the fund, and your personal contribution. Every Shekel paid out came from a player donation.

:::tip[Quick check]
Run `/tzedakah balance` to see the amount available now. Run `/tzedakah stats` to see the current fund, the all-time donated total, and your own lifetime giving.
:::

## How the fund is tracked

Your lifetime donation total never goes down when staff spend money from the fund. For example, if you give 7 Shekels and staff later use 5 to help a new player, your own total still reads 7.

The community display separates three numbers:

| Display | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Community donated | All Shekels donated over time |
| Available fund | Shekels that staff can give out now |
| You have given | Your lifetime donation total |

## Placing your own box

If you own a Tzedakah Box, place it like a chest. A donation hologram appears above it automatically. Break the box to pick it up again.

## Shekel balance and Gold Shekel items

The Tzedakah Box uses your **balance**, which is the server's regular currency. Check it with `/balance`.

A **Gold Shekel** is a physical item that you can store or trade. `/deposit <amount|all>` turns Gold Shekel items into balance, and `/withdraw <amount>` turns balance into items. Ask staff for the current exchange rate because one Gold Shekel item can be worth more than 1 balance Shekel.
