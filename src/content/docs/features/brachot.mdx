Both brachos on this page come from **Sefer Brachos**, which you are given on your first login. See [Skills and Sefarim](/features/skills/).

## Modeh Ani

Sleep through a full night in a bed. While you are still in bed, or shortly after getting up, type the whole Modeh Ani line in chat:

```text
modeh ani lefanecha melech chai vekayam shehechezarta bi nishmati bchemla raba emunasecha
```

You earn [Kosher Points](/yidcraft/wiki/features/koshersystem) once each morning.

Transliteration does not need to be exact. These versions work:

| Word | Accepted forms |
|---|---|
| Modeh | `modeh`, `modah`, `mode` |
| Lefanecha | `lefanecha`, `lfanecha`, `l'fanecha`, `lifanecha` |
| Vekayam | `vekayam`, `v'kayam`, `vkayam`, `vekayaim` |
| Shehechezarta | `shehechezarta`, `sheh'he'chezarta` |
| Nishmati | `nishmati`, `nishmasi` |
| Emunasecha | `emunasecha`, `emunatecha`, `emunascha` |
| Raba | `raba`, `rabah`, `rabbah` |
| Melech | `melech`, `melekh` |

Capital letters and punctuation are ignored. One typo or one missing word is forgiven, and a failed attempt stays private. You will get a quiet message asking you to try again.

## The bracha in your chat

When you eat, the bracha appears in your chat, and only you see it:

```
✡ Bracha: Borei Pri Ha'etz
✡ After Bracha: Borei Nefashos
```

Hover over it for the full text. It follows the halacha, so it never tells you to make a bracha you do not need:

- **One bracha covers more of the same kind.** Three apples in a row: one Ha'etz.
- **Bread starts a meal.** Hamotzi covers everything else you eat in it, and at the end comes Birkas Hamazon.
- **The after bracha comes once you stop eating**, and only if you ate a kezayis (or drank a revi'is) quickly enough.
- **A new start.** Walking far away, changing world, sleeping or logging out ends it, and the next food gets a new bracha.

It is on for everyone who knows **Sefer Brachos**. `/kosher brachos off` turns it off, `/kosher brachos on` turns it back on, and your choice is remembered.

## The mezuzah bracha

A newly placed mezuzah is **not yet valid** until you say its bracha in normal chat:

```text
Baruch Ata Hashem Elokeinu Melech Haolam Asher Kidishanu B'mitzvotav V'tzivanu Likbo'a Mezuzah
```

Spelling is forgiving here too. `boruch`/`baruch`, `atah`/`ata`, and `bmitzvosav`/`b'mitzvotav` all work, and apostrophes do not matter.

**One word does have to be right: Elokeinu.** We say *Elokeinu* because the real Name of Hashem is not said outside of davening, so we use this form in its place. Type the actual Name and the bracha will not be accepted.

The full mezuzah system is on the [Mezuzos](/features/mezuzos/) page.
