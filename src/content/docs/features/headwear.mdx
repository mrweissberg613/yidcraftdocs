Four head coverings: a **Kippah** and a **Black Hat** for men, a **Tichel** and a **Sheitel** for married women. Put one on your head like a helmet.

| Item | On its own | Over a helmet |
|---|---|---|
| Kippah | 1 armor | the helmet's armor **+1** |
| Tichel | 1 armor | the helmet's armor **+1** |
| Black Hat | 2 armor | the helmet's armor **+2** |
| Sheitel | 2 armor | the helmet's armor **+2** |

## Dye them

A kippah and a tichel can be any colour. Put one in a crafting table with a dye, just like leather armor. The colour stays when you put it over a helmet.

## Over a helmet

Put any helmet and a head covering together in a crafting table. You get one item that looks like the kippah or hat, with a little trim of the helmet's metal, and is called by both: **Diamond Black Hat**, **Iron Kippah**, **Netherite Tichel**. It keeps everything the helmet had (its protection, enchantments and durability) and adds the head covering's armor on top.

<img src="/recipes/hat_over_helmet.png" alt="A diamond helmet and a black hat make a Diamond Black Hat" width="460" />

Leather, chainmail, copper, iron, gold, diamond, netherite and the turtle shell all work.

## Making them

All four need **Mishnah Berurah - Kisui HaRosh**. You find it in village chests (shepherds, tanneries and houses) and in stronghold libraries. Without it the crafting table stays empty and tells you so.

**Kippah**: any wool and a string.

<img src="/recipes/kippah.png" alt="Kippah: wool and string" width="460" />

**Tichel**: two wool and a string.

<img src="/recipes/tichel.png" alt="Tichel: two wool and string" width="460" />

**Black Hat**: three black wool and a leather.

<img src="/recipes/black_hat.png" alt="Black Hat: three black wool and leather" width="460" />

**Sheitel**: two wool, two string and a leather.

<img src="/recipes/sheitel.png" alt="Sheitel: two wool, two string and leather" width="460" />
