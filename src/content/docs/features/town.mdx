---
title: The Town and its People
description: Meet the residents of the town, use their shops, visit the mikveh, and do their weekly favours.
---

The town is full of people to meet. Walk up to anyone with a name over their head and **right click** them. They greet you (differently the first time, and differently once you are a regular), and then you pick what to talk about. Press the key the conversation shows (**Space**, jump) to move it on, and walk a few blocks away to end it.

Many of them run a shop, and several ask for a small favour once a week.

:::note[Homeless Yankel]
Come within a few blocks of him and he collars you: he will not let you go until you pay or log off. If you do not have 10 Shekels, he lets you go with a warning. He stops each player at most once every 10 minutes, so walking past him again is safe for a while.
:::

## Going into the mikveh

<img src="/assets/town-mikveh-reception.jpg" alt="Reb Yossi Weiss at the mikveh reception" width="800" />

The doors of the men's and the women's mikveh only open for someone who has paid at reception: **50 Shekels**. Ask Reb Yossi (men) or Mrs. Devorah Leah (women) "May I go in?". Each one opens their own door only. You then have 15 seconds to go in, and the door shuts behind you. When you are done, `/spawn` takes you back.

## Weekly favours

Some residents need a hand once a week. Ask them if they need anything; if you accept, bring what they ask for (all of it at once, in your inventory) and talk to them again.

| Who | Wants | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| Reb Berel | 12 wheat | A fresh challah and 5 Shekels |
| Mrs. Rosen | 8 string, 4 white wool and 2 black dye | 20 Shekels |
| Avrumi | 16 oak logs | 20 Shekels |
| Mrs. Bloom | 12 bone meal | A flower pot and 6 Shekels |
| Reb Shmuel | 12 paper, 2 ink sacs and a book | 25 Shekels |

Each favour can be done once a week.
