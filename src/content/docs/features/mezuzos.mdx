A mezuzah goes on the doorpost of your home. It protects the door, and it earns you Kosher Points over time.

## Putting one up

<Steps>

1. Buy a **Mezuzah** from `/kosher shop` for 150 Kosher Points.
2. Right-click the doorpost beside your door.

</Steps>

It is placed for you: the correct doorpost, the correct height, tilted the correct way for your minhag. You do not position it by hand.

<img src="/assets/mezuzah-doorpost.png" alt="A mezuzah affixed to the doorpost beside a door" width="700" />

`/mezuzah minhag <ashkenazi|sephardi>` sets whether yours hang slanted or upright. Ashkenazi mezuzos lean with the top pointing **into** the house.

:::note
Placing a mezuzah inside spawn protection is blocked on purpose, otherwise you would own a spawn door. Everything else — Tzedakah Boxes, mikvaos, sefarim, Jewish Libraries — works at spawn.
:::

## Say the bracha

A newly placed mezuzah is **not yet valid**. Type the bracha in normal chat:

```text
Baruch Ata Hashem Elokeinu Melech Haolam Asher Kidishanu B'mitzvotav V'tzivanu Likbo'a Mezuzah
```

Spelling does not need to be perfect. `boruch`/`baruch`, `atah`/`ata`, and `bmitzvosav`/`b'mitzvotav` all work, and apostrophes do not matter.

:::caution[One word does have to be right]
**Elokeinu.** Say the Name itself and the bracha will not be accepted.
:::

Until you say it, `/mezuzah house` counts the mezuzah as *unfixed*, and it does not count toward your home.

## Who can open your doors

| Who | Gets in |
|---|---|
| You | Always |
| Anyone you `/mezuzah add` | Yes, at **every door in that building** |
| Your team | Yes, unless you turn it off |
| Everyone else | No |

`/mezuzah add <player>` covers the whole building at once — let someone in the front door and they can open the bedroom too. `/mezuzah kick <player>` takes it back. `/mezuzah trusted` shows who can open the door you are looking at.

`/mezuzah team <true|false>` decides whether your [teammates](/features/teams/) get in. It also spreads across the building, but a door you set **individually** keeps its own answer, and later changes leave it alone. That is how you make everything open to your team except the vault.

`/mezuzah team follow` puts a door back in step with the rest of the building.

Leaving a team closes their doors to you immediately.

### Redstone does not get around it

A button, lever, or pressure plate counts as the action of whoever used it, and is checked exactly like a hand on the door. An item thrown onto a wooden plate counts as the action of whoever threw it.

A signal nobody can be blamed for — a hopper clock, an observer, a mob wandering onto a plate — is blocked.

## Touching the mezuzah

Sneak + right-click an affixed mezuzah to touch it. Once per cooldown that is a Kosher Point. Clicking repeatedly does nothing extra.

## Wear and repair

Mezuzos wear over time and eventually stop counting. A **Sofer's Quill** restores one, but only in the hands of someone who has studied **[Hilchos Stam](/features/skills/)**.

A sofer can repair other players' mezuzos, which is the service that skill exists to create.

## Checking your home

`/mezuzah house` scans the enclosed building you are standing in and reports required doorways, valid mezuzos, unfixed ones, and your completion.

## Removing one

Look directly at it and run `/mezuzah remove`. Owner or staff only.

## Command list

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/mezuzah` | Mezuzah command list |
| `/mezuzah add <player>` | Let someone into your whole home |
| `/mezuzah kick <player>` | Take that access back |
| `/mezuzah trusted` | Who can open the door you are looking at |
| `/mezuzah team <true\|false>` | Let your team in, or not |
| `/mezuzah team follow` | Put a door back in step with the building |
| `/mezuzah minhag <ashkenazi\|sephardi>` | Slanted or upright |
| `/mezuzah house` | Scan your home for missing mezuzos |
| `/mezuzah remove` | Remove the mezuzah you are looking at |

## Common questions

### My mezuzah does not count.

You probably have not said the bracha. `/mezuzah house` lists unfixed mezuzos separately.

### The mezuzah leans the wrong way.

Check `/mezuzah minhag`. If that is right and it still leans wrong, tell staff — it is a one-line server setting, not something you can fix.

### My teammate cannot open my door.

Check `/mezuzah team` at that door. A door set individually ignores building-wide changes.
