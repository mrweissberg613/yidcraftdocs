This category explains the main features available on Yidcraft.

## Small features

Some smaller features are documented in the basics page, including trading and starter navigation.

- **[Trading](/guides/basics/#trading)** is covered in Basics so you can quickly learn how to trade items and shekels.
- **[Spawn Commands](/guides/basics/#essential-commands)** such as `/town` and `/spawn` are also in Basics.
- **[Limtied Maces](/guides/basics/#limited-maces)** the limited maces Basics.


## Full list of features
- **[The Town](/features/town)** meet its residents, visit their shops and do weekly favours
- **[Homes](/features/homes)** learn how the homes system works
- **[Teams](/features/teams)** learn how the teams system works
- **[Brewing](/features/brewing)** learn how to brew wine
- **[Player Markets](/features/playermarket)** rent and decorate your own market space
- **[Player Shops](/features/chestshops)** run an NPC shop that trades while you are offline
- **[Kosher system](/features/koshersystem)** learn how the Kosher System works
- **[Skills and Sefarim](/features/skills)** learn halacha by finding and studying sefarim
- **[Mezuzos](/features/mezuzos)** put up a mezuzah and control who opens your doors
- **[Brachot](/features/brachot)** Modeh Ani and the mezuzah bracha
- **[Tzedakah](/features/tzedakah)** learn how the community fund works
- **[Custom Foods](/features/customfoods)** learn how to make custom Jewish foods
