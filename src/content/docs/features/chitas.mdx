---
title: Chitas Pass
description: A free daily pass. Take a gift every day, do three small tasks, collect stars and open thirty tiers of rewards each month.
---

The **Chitas Pass** is free for everyone. Type `/chitas` (or `/pass`) to open it. It looks like the Boss Journal: an old book, with your week on the left page and the rewards on the right.

## Stars

Every **10 stars** opens the next tier.

- **Today's gift.** Click it once a day: **3 stars** and **10 Shekels**. Seven days in a row gives **7 stars** on the seventh day.
- **Today's three tasks.** Everyone gets the same three, picked fresh each day:

| Task | Stars |
|---|---|
| Make 5 brachos before eating | 4 |
| Eat kosher food 5 times | 3 |
| Touch a mezuzah | 3 |
| Say Modeh Ani when you wake up | 3 |
| Put on tefillin | 5 |
| Wear your tzitzis or Chai | 4 |
| Do mivtzoim with 2 villagers | 5 |
| Give tzedakah | 4 |
| Win a trivia question | 5 |
| Help beat a boss | 8 |

## Rewards

Thirty tiers: Shekels, Kosher Points, honey cookies, challah, gefilte fish, Shabbos candles, XP bottles, emeralds, a name tag and golden carrots. Click a tier once you reach it to take its reward.

Tiers **10, 20 and 30** are a **Chitas Crate**. Open it for a handful of things at once: Shekels, Kosher Points, and maybe cookies, XP, emeralds, pomegranates, golden carrots or a name tag. Now and then there is a common sefer inside, one you have not learned yet. See [Crates](/features/bosses/#crates).

The pass starts over at the start of each month.

## On Bedrock

`/chitas` opens the same pass as a Bedrock window.
