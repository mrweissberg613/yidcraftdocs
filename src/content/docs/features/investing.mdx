:::tip[Investing System]
The Investing system allows players to purchase shares in companies, manage their portfolio, and watch companies rise and fall in value. Every investment has risk, so invest carefully.
:::

Investing allows players to participate in the YidCraft economy by buying company shares, tracking their wealth, and making strategic decisions based on market changes.

---

## Viewing Companies

To browse all available companies, use:

```text
/invest companies
```

This command opens a list of all companies currently available for investment.

<img src="/blockstreet/companies showcase.png" alt="Companies Showcase" width="700" />

:::tip[Investment Tip]
Before investing, compare different companies and check their current value.
:::

---

## Company Information

Selecting a company opens a detailed company menu where you can view:

- Current stock price
- Company information
- Available shares
- Buying and selling options

<img src="/blockstreet/companie_showcase_gui.png" alt="Company Information" width="700" />

:::tip[Market Changes]
Company values can change over time. Always check the current price before buying or selling shares.
:::

---

## Your Portfolio

Your portfolio allows you to manage and track your investments.

You can view:

- Owned company shares
- Current investment value
- Profits and losses
- Overall portfolio performance

<img src="/blockstreet/portfolio.png" alt="Portfolio" width="700" />

:::tip[Portfolio Advice]
Spreading investments across multiple companies can reduce risk compared to investing everything into one company.
:::

---

## Bankrupt Companies

Not every company succeeds. Companies that fail are moved to the bankrupt companies list and can no longer be traded.

<img src="/blockstreet/bankrupt companies.png" alt="Bankrupt Companies" width="700" />

:::danger[Investment Risk]
Investments can lose value. A company going bankrupt may result in losing your investment.
:::

---

## Investment Tips

:::tip[Diversify]
Spread your investments across multiple companies instead of relying on one investment.
:::

:::tip[Monitor Prices]
Stock prices change over time. Check company values before buying or selling.
:::

:::tip[Be Patient]
Long-term investments can perform better than constantly buying and selling.
:::

:::tip[Watch The Market]
Keep an eye on new companies and market events announced by the server.
:::

---

## Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/invest` | Opens the main investing menu. |
| `/invest help` | Displays the investing help menu. |
| `/invest portfolio` | Opens your investment portfolio. |
| `/invest buy` | Purchase shares in a company. |
| `/invest sell` | Sell shares that you own. |
| `/invest companies` | View all available companies. |
| `/invest company` | View detailed information about a company. |
| `/invest notification` | Toggle investment notifications on or off. |
