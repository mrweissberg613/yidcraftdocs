Knowledge on YidCraft is permanent. There are no levels, no XP, and no grinding. You either know a halacha or you do not.

Run `/skills` to open your knowledge menu. Green means learned, grey means not.

<img src="/assets/skills-menu.png" alt="The /skills menu, one icon per skill" width="558" />

## The skills

| Skill | What it lets you do | How you get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Hilchos Shechita** | Use the [Shechita Knife](/features/koshersystem/#shechita-and-the-shechita-knife) | Given on your first login |
| **Sefer Brachos** | Modeh Ani, and the [mezuzah bracha](/features/mezuzos/#say-the-bracha) | Given on your first login |
| **Advanced Shechita** | More meat from every proper shechita, and kosher hide from cows | Found while exploring |
| **Hilchos Mikvaos** | Build your own [private mikveh](/features/koshersystem/#building-your-own-mikveh) | Found while exploring |
| **Hilchos Stam** | Repair mezuzos, yours and other people's, and copy a sefer | Found while exploring |
| **Hilchos Tefillin** | Make klaf, write the parshiyos and put [tefillin](/features/mitzvos/#tefillin-1) together. Learn Hilchos Stam first | Found while exploring, very rare |
| **Hilchos Tzitzis** | Make a tallis katan, and tie tzitzis onto a chestplate or leggings | Found while exploring |
| **Hilchos Kisui HaRosh** | Make a [kippah, black hat, tichel and sheitel](/features/headwear/), and wear them over a helmet | Found while exploring |
| **Melachas HaTzoref** | Make a [Chai necklace](/features/mitzvos/#the-chai-necklace-1), and hang it on a chestplate | Found while exploring |
| **Hilchos Asakim** | Search every player shop with [`/shopsearch`](/features/chestshops/#shop-search) | Found while exploring |

You start with the first two, so nothing is gated behind a hunt on day one.

When you try a craft you have not learned, the crafting table stays empty and the bar above your hotbar names the sefer you need.

## Sefarim

Every skill is taught by a physical sefer, a real book you can read. **Right-click it** to study. The pages open, and you learn the halachos inside.

### A sefer teaches only once

After someone studies it, the sefer becomes a keepsake. The pages still open, its cover says who read it, and it will never teach again.

That is worth knowing for **Shulchan Aruch - Hilchos Mikvaos** in particular. Its later pages are a step-by-step guide to the construction wand, so keep the sefer rather than selling it and you have the instructions on hand.

So a sefer you find is genuinely yours. Nobody can borrow it, learn from it, and hand it back.

You can still keep it, shelve it, sell it, or trade it as a collectible.

:::caution
Renaming an ordinary book in an anvil does nothing. The plugin checks hidden item data, not the name.
:::

### Where the rare sefarim are

They are not sold in any shop. You find them exploring:

| Sefer | Look in |
|---|---|
| Advanced Shechita | Dungeons, mineshafts, desert pyramids, jungle temples, outposts |
| Shulchan Aruch - Hilchos Mikvaos | Stronghold libraries, buried treasure, shipwrecks, ocean ruins |
| Hilchos Stam | Stronghold libraries, woodland mansions, ancient cities, bastions, ruined portals, end cities |
| Mishnah Berurah - Hilchos Tefillin | Stronghold libraries, woodland mansions, ancient cities, end cities |
| Shulchan Aruch - Hilchos Tzitzis | Village weaponsmiths, shepherds and tanneries, stronghold libraries |
| Mishnah Berurah - Kisui HaRosh | Village shepherds, tanneries and houses, stronghold libraries |
| Melachas HaTzoref | Village toolsmiths and weaponsmiths, desert pyramids, stronghold libraries |
| Choshen Mishpat - Hilchos Asakim | Village cartographers, shipwrecks, buried treasure, desert pyramids, stronghold libraries |

They are rare on purpose, roughly one chest in twenty at best, and Hilchos Tefillin far rarer. A sefer is a find, not a farm.

Two more places to find one:

- **Bosses.** The False Scholar often drops a sefer you have not learned yet, and the other bosses sometimes do. See [Boss Hunting](/features/bosses/).
- **The Chitas Crate** from the [Chitas Pass](/features/chitas/) sometimes holds one of the common sefarim.

### Copying a sefer

Someone who learned **Hilchos Stam** can write a copy of a sefer they learned: the sefer, a book and **Ink of Truth** from the False Scholar. You get two fresh copies, and each one can teach someone.

## The Jewish Library

Buy a **Jewish Library** from `/kosher shop` for 300 Kosher Points.

<Steps>

1. Place the library like any block.
2. Right-click an empty slot to shelve a sefer.
3. Right-click an occupied slot to take it back.

</Steps>

It holds six sefarim, and the books show on the shelf.

**It only accepts Jewish books.** Ordinary and enchanted books belong on a normal bookshelf, and the library will say so.

Break the library and everything on it drops.

## Common questions

### I studied a sefer and now it says "Studied by me".

That is right. It taught you, and it will not teach anyone else. Keep it, shelve it, or sell it as a collectible. You keep the knowledge either way.

### Someone gave me a sefer and it did nothing.

Somebody already studied that copy. You need an unread one.

### I bought a Mikveh Wand and it does nothing.

You need **Hilchos Mikvaos**. Buying the tool and knowing the halachos are separate things, so find and study the sefer. Once you have, the sefer's later pages walk you through working the wand, and `/mikveh wand` hands you one for free.

### I cannot repair a mezuzah with the Sofer's Quill.

You need **Hilchos Stam**, or ask a player who has it.
