Run `/kosher keep` to join the Kosher Challenge. From then on, kosher food earns **Kosher Points**, properly prepared meat pays the most, and forbidden food costs you points, health, and a chance.

## Quick start

1. Run `/kosher keep`.
2. Craft a **Shechita Knife** with an iron sword, wheat, and bone stacked vertically.
3. Go to the mikveh at spawn. Stand in the water and click while holding the knife to toivel it.
4. Kill a cow with the knife, cook the beef, and eat it for points.

:::tip[Useful commands]
`/kosher` lists the commands. `/kosher status` shows your points, chances, and fleishig timer. Use `/kosher points` for your point total, `/kosher shop` to spend points, and `/kosher notifications` to mute or unmute point messages.
:::

You do not need to find anything first. **Hilchos Shechita** and **Sefer Brachos** are given to you on your first login, so the knife and the mezuzah bracha work right away. See [Skills and Sefarim](/features/skills/) for the rest.

## The Kosher Challenge

The system only applies after you run `/kosher keep`. Once you have joined:

- Kosher food earns Kosher Points.
- Food prepared the wrong way earns nothing, but costs nothing.
- Inherently forbidden food costs points, health, and one chance.

You start with **3 chances**. Losing all of them resets your Kosher Points and starts a cooldown before you can join again. Check `/kosher status` whenever you need the current count.

### Penalties

| What you did | Kosher Points | Damage |
|---|---:|---|
| Ate something non-kosher | -10 | 2 hearts |
| Ate dairy while still fleishig | -5 | 1 heart |

The damage **ignores armour**. Diamond gear is not a defence against what you ate.

You never lose more points than you actually have, so your total cannot go below zero. Eating dairy early costs points and health, but it does not use up a chance.

### Always forbidden

Pig, rabbit, horse, rotten flesh, spider eyes, and anything from the water without fins and scales. That covers pufferfish and tropical fish. No preparation makes these kosher.

### Fine as they are

Bread, fruit, vegetables, eggs, dairy, cod, and salmon need no special preparation. Fish are not slaughtered, so cooked cod and salmon count as kosher straight out of the water.

### Meat takes work

Beef, mutton, and chicken only earn points when the animal was shechted. Meat from a kosher animal killed with an ordinary sword costs no chances, but it scores nothing.

## Shechita and the Shechita Knife

You need **Hilchos Shechita**, which you already have, and a **Shechita Knife**. Craft it in a 3x3 crafting grid: iron sword, wheat, and bone vertically in the middle column.

<img src="/assets/kniferecipe.png" alt="Crafting recipe for the Shechita Knife" width="400" height="400" />

Hold the knife in your main hand and use it to kill a cow, mooshroom, sheep, goat, or chicken. Pigs and rabbits never work, and the knife will tell you why. Leather, wool, feathers, bones, and Looting drops still work normally.

### One clean cut

Shechita is a single uninterrupted stroke, so the knife **kills any animal in one hit**. No hacking an animal down over five swings.

This works on animals only. Against a zombie or another player the knife does exactly what an iron sword does. It is a slaughter tool, not a better weapon.

### The rules

- Kill with the knife in your **main hand**
- The animal must be a kosher species
- The knife should be **toiveled**, or the meat is marked and earns nothing

A Shechita Knife cannot make a non-kosher species kosher. Killing a pig with it just kills a pig.

The dropped meat carries hidden kosher data. Renaming it does not change that data, and cooking it in a furnace keeps the kosher status. Raw meat is worth nothing, so cook it before eating unless your knife has Bishul.

### Advanced Shechita

If you have found and studied the **Advanced Shechita** sefer, a proper shechita yields **double the meat**. It multiplies what the animal already dropped, so Looting still matters and a chicken is still worth less than a cow. See [Skills and Sefarim](/features/skills/) for where that sefer is found.

## Tevilah at the mikveh

New metal utensils need **tevilah**, which means immersion in a mikveh. An undipped knife still produces meat with kosher data, but the meat earns no points.

<img src="/assets/mikveh.png" alt="The mikveh at YidCraft spawn" width="700" />

### How to toivel an item

<Steps>

1. Go to spawn and find the mikveh pool.
2. Hold the item in your main hand.
3. Stand **inside** the water.
4. Left-click, right-click, or sneak. Any of the three works.

</Steps>

Particles, a sound, and a chat message confirm that it worked. The item's lore will change to `Tevilah: Toiveled`.

You are always told what happened. If you stand beside the water, the plugin tells you to step in, and `/mikveh here` reports exactly what the plugin sees where you are standing. Players cannot place blocks inside the mikveh, so the pool cannot be drained by accident.

### Items that need tevilah

| Tools and vessels |
|---|
| Shechita Knife |
| Furnace, Smoker, and Blast Furnace |
| Bowls, Buckets, and Glass Bottles |
| Shears and Cauldrons |

New items show their current status in the lore:

```text
Tevilah: Needs tevilah     <- take it to the mikveh
Tevilah: Toiveled          <- ready to use
```

### Building your own mikveh

With **Hilchos Mikvaos** you can build a mikveh yourself and offer it to other players. The sefer that teaches the skill also teaches the procedure, so you can re-read it in game whenever you forget a step.

<Steps>

1. Get a **Mikveh Wand**. Run `/mikveh wand` if you have the skill, or buy one from `/kosher shop`, which sells them to anybody.
2. Dig and fill your pool.
3. **Left-click** one corner, then **right-click** the far corner. Each click confirms the coordinates it recorded.
4. Run `/mikveh create <name>`.

</Steps>

Buying the wand and knowing how to use it are separate things. Without the skill the wand marks nothing, and it tells you why. That is deliberate: the wand is an ordinary tradeable item, so you can buy one as a gift for a player who has studied the halachos.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/mikveh wand` | Get a selection wand |
| `/mikveh create <name>` | Save the two corners as a mikveh |
| `/mikveh list` | Every mikveh on the server |
| `/mikveh remove <name>` | Delete one you built |
| `/mikveh here` | Why tevilah is or is not working where you stand |

You can remove a mikveh you built. You cannot remove anyone else's, including the public one at spawn.

## Bishul

**Bishul** is a special enchantment for the Shechita Knife. Combine a Bishul book with the knife in an anvil. The anvil costs XP levels.

<img src="/assets/bishul-knife.png" alt="A Shechita Knife showing the Bishul enchantment and Toiveled status" width="576" />

A knife with Bishul drops meat already cooked. The kosher marking carries over, so the result is the same as slaughtering the animal and then smelting the raw meat. You can eat the drop right away and earn the normal cooked-meat points.

It is a cooking enchantment, not a kashrus one. Non-kosher meat stays non-kosher, just cooked.

The item lore can show `Bishul I` together with `Tevilah: Toiveled`. Bishul only works on a Shechita Knife. It cannot be moved onto an ordinary sword, and a grindstone will not remove it. Ask staff how to get a Bishul book.

## Fleishig and milchig

Eating meat or poultry makes you **fleishig**. Dairy is off the table until the clock runs out, and eating it early costs **5 Kosher Points and a heart**. Wait for the timer to finish or eat something pareve.

`/kosher status` shows the time left:

```text
Status: Fleishig - dairy costs points for 14m
Status: Pareve - you may eat dairy
```

### Milk does not rescue you

Milk normally clears every potion effect, the universal cure. Drink it while you are still fleishig and **the bucket empties but the effects come straight back**. You drank it, it did nothing, and you are told why.

Modeh Ani earns Kosher Points too. It has its own page: [Brachot](/features/brachot/).

## Kosher Points and rewards

Points come from eating properly prepared kosher food, from having a valid [mezuzah](/features/mezuzos/), from touching a mezuzah, and from [Modeh Ani](/features/brachot/).

These are the usual food values:

| Food | Points |
|---|---:|
| Fruit, vegetables, bread, and cookies | 1-2 |
| Cooked cod or salmon | 4 |
| Matzah, Challah, or Gefilte Fish | 5-10 |
| Cooked kosher chicken | 12 |
| Cooked kosher mutton | 15 |
| Cooked kosher beef | 18 |

Properly shechted meat is the fastest earner. Apples will not get you very far.

### Milestones

Each milestone gives a one-time Shekel payment. `/kosher status` shows your progress.

| Points | Title | Reward |
|---:|---|---:|
| 500 | Shomer Kashrus | 250 Shekels |
| 2,500 | Ben Torah | 1,000 Shekels |
| 7,500 | Mehadrin | 2,500 Shekels |
| 20,000 | Mashgiach | 7,500 Shekels |
| 50,000 | Gadol Hador | 20,000 Shekels |

## Tzedakah and custom foods

The [Tzedakah system](/features/tzedakah/) lets you donate Shekels to a shared fund that helps new players. A Tzedakah Box accepts one Shekel each time you right-click it.

Matzah, Challah, and Gefilte Fish are all kosher custom foods. They count as prepared food and award Kosher Points. See [Custom Foods](/features/customfoods/) for their recipes.

## Common questions

### Why will my kosher meat not stack with other meat?

Kosher meat, non-kosher meat, and meat cut with an undipped knife are tracked separately. Two pieces with the same status stack normally.

### Why did cooked beef give me nothing?

The animal was not shechted. Kill it with a Shechita Knife, and make sure the knife was toiveled.

### Why does my new furnace say "Needs tevilah"?

Cooking vessels need dipping too. Take the furnace to the mikveh at spawn, stand in the water, and click while holding it.

### Why did dairy cost me points and a heart?

You were still fleishig. Run `/kosher status` to see the timer.

### Does anything work inside spawn protection?

Yes. Tzedakah Boxes, mikvaos, sefarim, and Jewish Libraries all work inside spawn protection. Placing a **mezuzah** at spawn is still blocked on purpose, otherwise you would own a spawn door.

### Why does the GUI look like a plain chest?

You have not accepted the server resource pack.

### Can I turn off the point messages?

Yes. Run `/kosher notifications`.
