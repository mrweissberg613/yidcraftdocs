YidCraft has three custom foods. Each one counts as kosher prepared food and earns Kosher Points after you join the challenge with `/kosher keep`, so these recipes are worth learning early.

## Matzah

<img src="/recipes/matzah.png" alt="Matzah crafting recipe" width="400" height="400" />

Craft Matzah with 1 wheat and 1 water bucket.

## Challah

<img src="/recipes/challah.png" alt="Challah crafting recipe" width="400" height="400" />

Craft Challah with 3 eggs, 1 water bucket, and 3 wheat. The in-game recipe book shows the slot layout.

## Gefilte Fish

<img src="/recipes/fish.png" alt="Gefilte Fish crafting recipe" width="400" height="400" />

Craft Gefilte Fish with 2 cooked cod.

:::tip
Custom foods are ready to eat when crafted. They do not need shechita or tevilah.
:::
