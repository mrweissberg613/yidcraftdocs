YidCraft has its own foods. Each one counts as kosher prepared food and earns Kosher Points after you join the challenge with `/kosher keep`, so these recipes are worth learning early.

<img src="/recipes/ozen_haman.png" alt="Ozen Haman crafting recipe: wheat round cocoa beans" width="460" />

Craft three Ozen Haman with 6 wheat and 1 cocoa bean, the dough around a chocolate filling. The bracha is **Mezonos**.

## Pomegranates

Break the leaves of an acacia tree, or let them fall, and now and then a **Rimon** drops. Only a tree's own leaves bear fruit, not leaves someone placed. Before: **Borei pri ha'etz**. After: **Al ha'etz**, because the pomegranate is one of the seven species.

## Grapes

Break vines and sometimes a bunch of **Grapes** drops. Before: **Borei pri ha'etz**. After: **Al ha'etz**, a seven species fruit.

## Grape juice

<img src="/recipes/grape_juice.png" alt="Grape juice crafting recipe: four grapes and a glass bottle" width="460" />

Craft Grape Juice with 4 bunches of grapes and a glass bottle. Before: **Borei pri hagafen**. After: **Al hagefen**.

## Wine

Put grape juice in a **barrel** and close it. After 20 minutes, open the barrel and the juice has become **Wine**. Same brachos as grape juice: **Borei pri hagafen** and **Al hagefen**.

The game tells you the right bracha before and after you eat, only in your chat. See [Brachot](/features/brachot/).

:::tip
Custom foods are ready to eat when crafted. They do not need shechita or tevilah.
:::
