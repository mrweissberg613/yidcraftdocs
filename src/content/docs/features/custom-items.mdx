### Pomegranates

Break the leaves of an acacia tree, or let them fall, and now and then a **Rimon** drops. Only a tree's own leaves bear fruit, not leaves someone placed. Before: **Borei pri ha'etz**. After: **Al ha'etz**, because the pomegranate is one of the seven species.

Put grape juice in a **barrel** and close it. After 20 minutes, open the barrel and the juice has become **Wine**. Same brachos as grape juice: **Borei pri hagafen** and **Al hagefen**.

## Kippah, Hats, Tichel, Sheitel and Tzitzis

Five head and clothing items: a **Kippah** and a **Black Hat** for men, a **Tichel** and a **Sheitel** for married women, and **Tzitzis** worn with a chestplate or leggings. Head coverings can be worn like helmets, while Tzitzis can be combined with chestplates or leggings.

| Item | On its own | Over a helmet / Armor |
|---|---|---|
| Kippah | 1 armor | the helmet's armor **+1** |
| Tichel | 1 armor | the helmet's armor **+1** |
| Black Hat | 2 armor | the helmet's armor **+2** |
| Sheitel | 2 armor | the helmet's armor **+2** |
| Tzitzis | — | the chestplate or leggings' armor **+1** |

All four need **Mishnah Berurah - Kisui HaRosh**. You find it in village chests (shepherds, tanneries and houses) and in stronghold libraries. Without it the crafting table stays empty and tells you so.

### Dye them

A kippah and a tichel can be any colour. Put one in a crafting table with a dye, just like leather armor. The colour stays when you put it over a helmet.

### Over a helmet

Put any helmet and a head covering together in a crafting table. You get one item that looks like the kippah or hat, with a little trim of the helmet's metal, and is called by both: **Diamond Black Hat**, **Iron Kippah**, **Netherite Tichel**. It keeps everything the helmet had (its protection, enchantments and durability) and adds the head covering's armor on top.

      ],
    },

/>

Leather, chainmail, copper, iron, gold, diamond, netherite and the turtle shell all work.

### Buying them

Reb Mendel's Kosher Shop sells all four:

| Item | Kosher Points |
|---|---|
| Kippah | 40 |
| Tichel | 40 |
| Black Hat | 250 |
| Sheitel | 250 |

On Bedrock they look the same on your head, and the helmet versions show their metal trim too.
