## Trivia

Every 20 minutes, when at least two players are online, a question appears in chat. About brachos, kashrus, the chagim, mitzvos and the stories of Tanach. The answer is shown with its source.

- **On Java**, click an answer in chat.
- **On Bedrock**, type `/trivia` and pick an answer in the window.
- You can also type `/trivia A` (or B, C, D).

One guess each. The first right answer wins **25 Shekels**. You have 40 seconds.

## Achievements

Achievements count the things you do again and again. Type `/achievements` (or `/ach`) to see yours. On Java they are also a **YidCraft** tab in the Advancements screen (press L), with a toast when you earn one.

`/achievements` opens a menu that works on Bedrock too, and you get a message when you earn one.
